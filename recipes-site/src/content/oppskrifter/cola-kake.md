52 Cola kake

200 gr smor (400 gr / 600 gr) - Rorast lett
200 gr sukker (400 gr / 600 gr)
300 gr kv. mjol (600 gr / 900 gr)
2 ts vaniljesukker (4 ts / 6 ts)
2 ts natron (4 ts / 6 ts)
1 ss sirup (2 ss / 3 ss)

Lag 4 stk brod. Steikast ca 10 min. Skjerast over i stk.
